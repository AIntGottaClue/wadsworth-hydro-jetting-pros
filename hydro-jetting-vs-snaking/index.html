Hydro jetting vs snaking for Wadsworth drains

A snake bores through a clog. A jet washes the pipe wall. Which one fits depends on what is blocking the line.

What a snake does well

A cable machine can open a drain fast and is common for a simple blockage. The city's handbook describes electric rodding with a root cutter as the first step for roots in a service line, according to the Wadsworth Sanitary Sewer Backup Handbook.

What a jet adds

Water jetting scours the inside of the pipe. That can remove grease and sludge that a cable only pokes a hole through. It is a poor fit for a broken pipe, because pressure can make a bad section worse.

How to decide

If the same drain keeps clogging after snaking, ask whether buildup is left behind. A camera can show what a cable missed and help match the tool to the pipe.

Questions about this topic

Is jetting better than snaking?

Neither is better in every case. Snaking often fits a single obstruction, and jetting fits buildup along the pipe wall.

Can a snake damage a pipe?

Any tool can harm a fragile line if used poorly. That is another reason to see the pipe on camera.

Do I need both?

Sometimes. A line may be opened with a cable and then cleaned with water to remove what remains.
