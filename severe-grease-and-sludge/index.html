Grease and sludge in Wadsworth kitchen and main lines

Grease cools and sticks to the walls of a drain, so a line can narrow for years before it finally stops.

Why grease keeps coming back

Hot grease runs freely down a kitchen drain and then cools in the first cold stretch of pipe. Layer after layer builds up on the pipe wall, and the sink may still drain, just more slowly each month. Plunging or a quick snake pass can open a hole through the middle without removing the coating.

Hydro jetting uses directed water to scrape that coating off suitable pipe. It works best when the pipe is sound and the nozzle fits the line.

Wadsworth buildings and old lines

Wadsworth's downtown is a National Register historic district, according to the Downtown Wadsworth Historic District page. Kitchen lines in older buildings can include sections of different materials and ages, and some may have been patched over the decades.

Before cleaning a heavily greased line, a camera can show where the grease is thickest and whether the pipe wall is intact. Cleaning a weak section hard is a poor bet.

Keeping grease out of the sewer

The city's sewer system is separate from storm drains and is built to carry household wastewater to its treatment plant, according to the Wadsworth Sanitary Sewer Backup Handbook. Grease that hardens in a private line can still back up into a basement floor drain. Wipe pans before washing and keep fats out of the sink.

A good question to ask

Ask where the grease is building up and whether the pipe is sound enough to be jetted.

Questions about severe grease and sludge

Why does my kitchen drain slow down again after I clear it?

A clog cleared with a plunger or snake may leave a coating on the pipe wall. That coating keeps narrowing the line until the drain slows again.

Can hydro jetting remove old grease?

It can remove grease buildup from a suitable pipe. A camera inspection first helps confirm the pipe can handle the pressure.

Will jetting stop grease from returning?

No cleaning lasts forever if grease keeps going down the drain. Changing kitchen habits matters as much as the cleaning itself.
