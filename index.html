Whose pipe is it?

The City of Wadsworth owns and runs a separate sanitary sewer system and its own wastewater treatment plant. The pipe from your house to that public sewer is a different matter. The city's Wadsworth Sanitary Sewer Backup Handbook says maintaining and repairing a building service line is the building owner's job.

A camera inspection can show where the trouble sits before anyone starts cleaning. That keeps you from paying to clear a private line when the problem is in the street, or the other way around.

Report first, then call

The city's backup handbook asks residents to report a sanitary sewer backup to the city as soon as it happens and before calling a plumber.

Older houses, mixed pipe

Wadsworth was settled in 1814, and by 1863 a railroad ran through the south end of town. Coal mining was the largest business for nearly 50 years, and the early 1900s brought match, injector, salt and brick companies that drew workers to town, according to the Main Street Wadsworth history.

Houses built for that workforce sit beside newer streets. The city handbook notes that many homes built before 1960 had foundation drains tied into the sanitary service, which can overload the sewer in heavy rain. Jetting cannot fix that, which is one more reason to look first.

Questions about Wadsworth drains

Does Wadsworth maintain the sewer line from my house?

The handbook from the city says the building service line is the owner's responsibility. The public main in the street is the city's. A camera inspection can show which side a problem is on.

Should I call the city or a plumber first for a backup?

The city asks that sanitary sewer backups be reported to it first. After that, a qualified professional can inspect the private line.

Can hydro jetting fix a cracked pipe?

No. It can remove buildup from a sound pipe, but a broken, offset or collapsed section needs repair.

Is hydro jetting right for an older Wadsworth house?

It depends on the condition of that pipe, not the age of the street. A camera inspection helps show whether the line is sturdy enough to jet.
