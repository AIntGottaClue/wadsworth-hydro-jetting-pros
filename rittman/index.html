Rittman sits where Wayne and Medina counties meet and was named for a railroad treasurer. It has its own water and sewer utility.

A railroad town with its own utilities

The City of Rittman says its first settlers took title to land in 1814 and that the town was named after the treasurer of the Baltimore and Ohio Railroad, whose line came through in the 1870s, according to the Rittman history. Early businesses included grist mills, tile-making, wagon-building and tobacco farming.

The city lists water and sewer on its own utilities page, so questions about the public sewer go there rather than to Wadsworth.

What this means for your drain

A house near the old railroad stops or the square may be well over a century old. A clay tile line from the early days can sit next to a newer plastic section after a repair. Camera inspection shows the mix.

Because Rittman straddles two counties, check which office handles a given property before assuming who to call about permits or septic records.

Questions about drains in Rittman

Who runs sewer service in Rittman?

The City of Rittman has a water and sewer utilities page. Property owners with a backup should contact the city utility.

Are Rittman houses older than Wadsworth ones?

Both areas were settled in the 1810s. The age of a particular house and its pipe depends on when it was built and repaired.

Can I get hydro jetting at a business in Rittman?

Hydro jetting is used on both homes and commercial buildings. A camera inspection tells you if the pipe can take it.
