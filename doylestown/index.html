A village on high ground

William Doyle laid out the village with 40 lots in 1827, and the village was incorporated in 1867 (Wikipedia on Doylestown, Wayne County Public Library wiki). The surrounding township was known as the great coal township of the county in an 1878 history, according to the Wayne County history.

A hilltop village drains differently from a river flat. Water runs off the slope, so heavy rain tends to move downhill to the lower streets.

Village utilities

The village runs a Water/Sewer Department and says residents and businesses are served through it, according to the Doylestown Water/Sewer Department. For a backup in the public system, start with the village.

For a slow drain inside a house, a camera inspection shows what is in the private line.

Questions about drains in Doylestown

Who handles sewer questions in Doylestown?

The village has a Water/Sewer Department. Call the village about the public system and a qualified professional for your private line.

Does the hilltop location affect drains?

Slope can change how storm water moves around a house. It does not change what is inside the pipe, so a camera is the sure way to know.

Is hydro jetting available for Doylestown homes?

Hydro jetting is used on home sewer lines when the pipe is sound enough. Inspection comes first.
